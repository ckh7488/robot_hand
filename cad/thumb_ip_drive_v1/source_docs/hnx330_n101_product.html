Features

Metal horn set for DYNAMIXEL XL330 and XC330 Series

High durability against repeated assembly and disassembly of DYNAMIXEL X330 during usage.

This horn set can be assembled on the output shaft wheel gear of DYNAMIXEL in order to assemble with frames such as a hinge or an adapter.
